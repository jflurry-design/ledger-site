Kitchen Table Finance

Spending log & monthly coach

A calm, private way for your household to see where your money goes. Log purchases in seconds, scan receipts on your iPhone, and get a plain-English monthly check-in against the goals you set.

Coming soon for iPhone

Log in seconds

Type an amount and a few letters of the store. The app suggests a category and learns from every change you make. Mark work expenses as reimbursable and track them until you're paid back.

Receipts read on your iPhone

Point the camera at a receipt and the app reads the store, date and total on the device. You check every field before anything is saved.

Share with your household

Invite the person you live with through iCloud and you both add to the same ledger. No accounts to create, and no servers of ours.

A monthly check-in

Set the goals you care about, such as spending caps, a savings target or a debt you're paying down. Each month you get a plain-English summary of how your spending compared, plus a few budgeting ideas to try.

Reports you own

Share a monthly PDF, export a spreadsheet-ready CSV, or export everything at any time, even if you stop subscribing.

At a glance

Widgets, a Control Center button, Siri and Shortcuts, and an optional month-status Live Activity.

A little extra help at first. Quieter once you subscribe.

New habits take a few days to stick, so during your free week the app checks in a bit more: an optional once-a-day reminder to log (it never shows amounts), a first coaching tip after about five entries, a sample monthly report to look through, and a “Your first week” summary on day 6.

Once you subscribe, monthly or yearly, the app gets quieter. Fewer reminders and prompts, no upsells, and only the notifications you've chosen. You can change or turn off reminders any time in Settings → Reminders.

Your data lives on your iPhone and in your iCloud.

No accounts, no servers, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. Receipt reading and the optional Apple Intelligence features run on your iPhone. We can't see what you enter.
