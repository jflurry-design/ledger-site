Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

The short version: we don't collect your data. Kitchen Table Finance has no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. What you enter stays on your iPhone and, if you use iCloud, in your own iCloud account. We can't see it.

This policy explains how Kitchen Table Finance (“the app”) handles information. The app is made by Jason Flurry, an individual developer (“we”, “us”).

What the app stores, and where

The app stores the things you put into it: expenses and other entries, categories, payment-method nicknames, goals, receipt photos, notes and your settings.

  • On your iPhone. Your data is saved in the app's storage on your device. Widgets and the optional Live Activity read a small summary from storage that is shared only between the app and its own widgets on the same iPhone.
  • In your own iCloud. If you're signed in to iCloud and allow the app to use it, your data syncs through Apple's CloudKit to your private iCloud database. That storage belongs to your Apple Account. We, the developer, have no access to it. Apple's handling of iCloud data is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • Nowhere else. We don't run servers, and the app doesn't send your data to us or to any third party.

Sharing with your household

If you invite someone to your household ledger, the app uses Apple's iCloud sharing. The people you invite can see and edit that shared ledger, including its receipt photos, and their changes sync back to you. You choose who to invite. The owner can remove people or stop sharing, and anyone you invite can leave at any time. Shared data is stored in iCloud under the owner's account, and we can't see it.

Receipts and the camera

  • The app asks for camera permission only when you choose to scan a receipt. When you pick a photo from your library, iOS hands over only the photo you pick; the app doesn't browse your library.
  • Text on a receipt is read on your iPhone using Apple's on-device text recognition. Receipt photos are saved with your entries, on your iPhone and in your iCloud if you use it.

Smart features (Apple Intelligence)

On iPhones that support Apple Intelligence, the app can optionally use Apple's on-device foundation model to help read receipts and to word your monthly check-in. This processing happens on your iPhone; the app doesn't send this content off the device. You can turn these features off in Settings → Smart features in the app, and everything still works without them.

Face ID, notifications, widgets and Siri

  • Face ID lock is handled by iOS. The app only learns whether unlocking succeeded and never receives your face data.
  • Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your iPhone. We don't run a notification server.
  • Widgets, Live Activities, Control Center, Siri and Shortcuts work with data that's already on your iPhone. Amounts are hidden on the Lock Screen by default.

Purchases

Subscriptions are sold and processed by Apple through the App Store. We don't receive your name, Apple Account details or payment information. The app checks your subscription status with Apple on your device.

Analytics, advertising and tracking

The app contains no third-party SDKs, analytics, advertising or crash-reporting services, and it doesn't track you across apps or websites. If you have turned on Share With App Developers in iOS settings, Apple may share crash reports and basic usage statistics with us. Apple provides these in a form that doesn't identify you, and you can turn this off at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements.

Exports

When you export a report, a CSV or everything, the app creates the files on your iPhone and hands them to the share sheet. Where they go next (Files, Mail, Messages and so on) is up to you, and those services' own privacy terms apply.

If you contact us

If you email us for support, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include. We use it only to reply to you, we don't add it to any mailing list, and we'll delete the conversation if you ask. Please don't send receipts or financial details unless they're needed to help you.

This website

This site is a set of static pages hosted by GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies, no analytics and no third-party scripts, and its fonts are served from this site. GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security and operations; see the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Your choices and deleting your data

  • Delete everything from the app: Settings → Delete all my data. If you own the ledger, this deletes it from your iPhone and your iCloud, for everyone you share it with. If you joined someone else's household, it removes you from that household and deletes the copy on your iPhone.
  • Deleting the app removes the data stored on your iPhone. Data already synced to iCloud stays in your iCloud until you delete it in the app first or manage it in iOS Settings → your name → iCloud.
  • Stop syncing by turning off iCloud for the app in iOS Settings. Your data then stays on your iPhone.
  • Take a copy at any time with Settings → Export everything, even without a subscription.

Because we don't hold your app data, we can't view, correct, export or delete it for you, and we can't recover it if it's deleted. The controls above put that in your hands. Privacy laws such as the GDPR and US state laws give you rights over personal data a company holds about you; apart from any support emails you send us, we don't hold any.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top. If a change would mean collecting any data, we'll say so clearly here and in the app before it takes effect.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email jflurry@me.com.
